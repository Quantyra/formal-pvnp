## Current Eq. (13) issue: exact scope

The current v1 still sets m = ceil(pi/(8 theta) - 1/2), initially theta = pi/4, and asserts success at least 1/2 when theta_a lies in [theta/2, theta]. Substitution theta_a = pi/8 gives m = 0 and success sin²(pi/8) = (2 - sqrt(2))/4, approximately 0.146447. This contradicts that stage guarantee. The same literal equation was previously visually checked in the PDF; today's HTML agrees.

It does **not** alone show that all preceding and subsequent stages fail the final probability bound, determine a corrected total query constant, or refute unknown-overlap amplification. In particular, the source's stated factor four cannot be treated as independently validated by this proof, but the local counterexample is not itself a replacement theorem. A corrected finite schedule, its full stopping distribution, all state preparations and checks, and its consequence for the source calculation would require separate analysis. The targeted current-version check found no arXiv revision repairing this step; that is not an exhaustive erratum search.

## Contribution decision and bounded missing evidence

**HOLD the combined paper.** The tail result can support an explicitly scoped reproducibility note; the gate audit can document a rejected implementation. Current evidence does not establish that either is a publishable substantive increment, and neither becomes a new theorem by being combined.

If this candidate is reconsidered, the most direct missing evidence is **source-functional alignment**, not a new simulation: identify exactly which input distribution, conditioning, randomization and cost functional a specified published prediction represents; then determine whether an already available, matched set of observations changes that prediction when evaluated using the same functional. The prerequisite is a frozen mapping from a particular source equation to one reproducible statistic and a matched baseline. Stop if this only reproduces the 2024 warning, depends on unsupported size/depth transfer, or lacks the necessary source data. No favorable conclusion should be forced from a finite n=20 penalty.

A separate possible assessment could ask whether the literal Eq. (13) defect changes the **final** stated constant after the complete schedule is accounted for, and whether a standard known schedule already resolves it. That is an alternative bounded gate, not an approved proof task or a presumed paper. No further research or publication is authorized by this note.

No claim about general polynomial-time SAT, NP versus BQP, P versus NP, hardware advantage or a new complexity separation follows.
