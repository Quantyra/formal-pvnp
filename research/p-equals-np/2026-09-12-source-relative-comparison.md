## Exact source law and preserved quantities

On a selected simple path component C of the source's H_low graph, with at most 22 edges, write W_uv=phi(t_u)phi(t_v), with the established phi, m2=3/32 and c=1/sqrt(5). The source law and proposed path law are

    A_C = 1 + epsilon sum_{e in C} W_e,
    P_C = product_{e in C}(1+epsilon W_e).               (1)

Use source parameter values for which A_C is a density; no assertion that an arbitrarily long additive component is positive is made. Components are independent. The clean comparison replaces only these selected path components and keeps every other component and placement law identical. In particular it keeps the source's TwoCC unary density, generated by gamma_T(r)=25r^3(1-2r)^2 below one half and zero above, unchanged. Roots in that class do not have uniform placements; their law must remain in every full expectation. Cyclic components cannot be replaced by the unnormalized path formula (1).

A_C and P_C have identical uniform vertex marginals and identical adjacent pair marginals 1+epsilon W_e. Consequently log P_C is a sum of functions whose expectations agree under A_C and P_C. With logarithms base two,

    KL(A_C||U)-KL(P_C||U) = KL(A_C||P_C) >= 0.          (2)

This is an exact comparison of the actual entropies, not two upper bounds. It is the fixed-tree maximum-entropy principle, not a novelty claim; the dependence-tree precedent is [Chow and Liu (1968), institutional primary record](https://research.ibm.com/publications/approximating-discrete-probability-distributions-with-dependence-trees). Equation (2) is proved directly by the edge-marginal identity here. Independence adds the identity over replaced components, and unchanged unary factors cancel.

For any observable G, including the sum of the finite cut indicators or the actual bounded-implication forced indicators, replacing a single component while holding the others fixed changes the objective E G-KL by exactly

    g_C + KL(A_C||P_C),
    g_C = E_{P_C x D_rest}G - E_{A_C x D_rest}G.        (3)

No independent-source surplus is discarded in (3). Its missing sign cannot be resolved from the edge marginals alone.

## A fully costed aggregate-CCT failure of the unrestricted replacement claim

Consider the six-variable uniquely satisfying at-most-3-CNF

    (x OR not a OR not b), (a OR not c), (b OR not d),
    (y OR not b OR not c), (c), (d).                    (4)

Its unique assignment is all ones. Choose the canonical critical clauses displayed in (4). The sibling graph includes ab from x and bc from y; select the two-edge path a-b-c, with other labels unmodified. At common CCT height two, the actual cut events are

    Cut_x = (A OR C)(B OR D),
    Cut_y = B OR D,
    Cut_a = Cut_b = Cut_c = Cut_d = true.              (5)

The letters in (5) are threshold bits relative to each event's own root placement. An expanded unit branch has no safe path. This is the actual shared-label CCT convention, not an independent-copy replacement.

For the two-edge path, P-A=epsilon^2 phi(a)phi(b)^2 phi(c). With M2(r)=integral_0^r phi^2 and gamma(r)=integral_0^r phi,

    P(Cut_x|t_x=r)-A(Cut_x|t_x=r)
       = -epsilon^2 gamma(r)^2 [r m2+(1-r)M2(r)].      (6)

Indeed integrating (A OR C) against phi(a)phi(c) gives -gamma^2; integrating (B OR D) against phi(b)^2 and the independent d placement gives M2+r(m2-M2). Cut_y depends only on b,d, whose joint marginal is unchanged. The other four events are constant. Therefore (6), integrated over r, is the difference of the **entire six-root cut sum**, with no omitted root correction. It obeys

    g <= -epsilon^2 m2 integral_0^(1/2) r gamma(r)^2 dr
       = -epsilon^2 * 3/71680.                        (7)

Here integral r gamma^2=1/2240. All roots x,y with nonconstant cuts are independent of the changed component.

The entropy saving starts at fourth order. To make the comparison finite rather than merely asymptotic, let Delta=P-A. For 0<epsilon<=.1, both A and P are at least .9 on this two-edge component. Also integral Delta^2=epsilon^4 m2^2 m4<=epsilon^4 m2^3. The standard log bound ln z<=z-1 gives

    KL(A||P) <= (1/ln 2) integral (A-P)^2/P
              <= epsilon^4 * 45/32768.                (8)

We used ln2>=2/3 and .9 in the denominator. At epsilon<=.1, epsilon^4*45/32768 < epsilon^2*3/71680. Equations (3), (7) and (8) prove that product replacement strictly worsens the aggregate height-two cut-minus-entropy objective throughout this interval.

Scope is essential. This easy formula does **not** meet the selected H_low interpretation: source one-shot pair closure derives the unit a from (a OR not c),(c) and the unit b from (b OR not d),(d). These give a,b additional critical clauses, putting them in TwoCC; both chosen edges meet that excluded set. Its six clauses force the whole solution at sufficient bounded implication, so actual forcing may be one for every variable, and its actual forcing-minus-entropy comparison is not the cut comparison above. It refutes only an unrestricted claim that source and product laws with the same canonical sibling edges universally preserve the all-root CCT certificate. It is neither a SAT-hard family nor a counterexample to improved PPSZ on the properly classified source class.

## Testing interpolation after full replacement fails

The attempt did not stop at the replacement sign. For one component set

    Q_lambda = A + lambda(P-A),  0<=lambda<=1,          (9)

with every other source factor unchanged. Its edge marginals remain fixed. With G as in (3), the exact objective derivative at lambda=0 is

    J'(0) = g_C + KL(A||P)+KL(P||A).                   (10)

To verify this, differentiate entropy and use integral(P-A)log P=0. This preserves the full actual source value J(0), including any surplus. For the cut diagnostic (4)-(7), positive interpolation also fails throughout the interval, not only at its endpoints. At each lambda,

    -d KL(Q_lambda||U)/d lambda
      = integral Delta (log P-log Q_lambda)
      <= integral Delta^2/[.9 ln2]
      <= epsilon^4*45/32768,                          (11)

using the mean-value theorem for log, min(P,Q_lambda)>=.9, and P-Q_lambda=(1-lambda)Delta. The expected cut derivative is the constant g from (7), strictly more negative than (11). Thus J'(lambda)<0 for all 0<=lambda<=1 in this diagnostic.

Allowing a small negative lambda is possible when A is bounded away from zero. It reverses the adverse direction in this example. In general, if d=J'(0), the objective is concave along the line and

    J''(lambda)=-(1/ln2) integral Delta^2/Q_lambda.

A feasible interval on which Q_lambda>=A/2 gives |J''|<=2 integral Delta^2/A divided by ln2. Choosing the sign of a sufficiently small lambda to match a nonzero d certifies a positive local increment by Taylor's theorem. This is generic entropy variational optimization, not a new SAT mechanism. It supplies no source-class lower bound on |d|, no linear-in-n sum of gains, and no protection against d=0. Computing or naming these derivatives does not establish such a bound. This fallback is therefore not selected as a new improvement route.

Extending component-local zero-marginal perturbations to cycles removes the algebraic restriction to paths but gives the same unresolved all-root derivative/sign problem. No cycle formula or entropy advantage is claimed without the required normalization and marginal checks.

## A source-classified family with no replaceable path components

The source reviewer supplied a separate structural test, independently checked by the proof reviewer. This one addresses actual source classification, not the cut-sign claim. Let L>=3 and take x_(i,j) for i modulo L and j modulo 3. Include the critical clauses

    C_(i,j) = x_(i,j) OR not x_(i+1,j) OR not x_(i+1,j+1),

and every positive clause on three distinct variables. There are n=3L variables and n+binomial(n,3) explicit clauses, a polynomial-size family.

The unique assignment is all ones. The positive clauses force the zero set to have size at most two. If any variable is zero, its critical clause requires a zero in the next cyclic group, so a zero set must visit all L groups. This contradicts size at most two.

Each variable has exactly one original critical clause. The source Definition 31 one-shot pair closure introduces no additional critical at-most-three clause: a useful resolution between two critical clauses has four distinct literals for L>=3; a useful critical/positive resolution retains at least two positive literals; positive clauses do not resolve with each other. Mixed pairs can yield new width-three **noncritical** clauses, which are not being denied. This is the source's one-shot pair inference on the original formula, not an iterated closure using newly added clauses. Thus TwoCC is empty in that source classification.

The sibling graph consists exactly of L disjoint triangles, one in each cyclic group. All variables have the canonical critical clause just specified. For distinct same-group labels y,z, z cannot occur in the canonical CCT rooted at y until depth L; the number of occurrences at depth d is at most 2^d. The source label-density expression therefore obeys, at every finite height,

    LabelDensity(z,T_y)
      = integral_0^(1/2) [(1-2r)^2/(1-r)^3]
             sum_{canonical v with label z} r^(depth(v)+1) dr
      <= integral_0^(1/2) 4(1-2r)(2r)^L dr
      = 2/[(L+1)(L+2)].                              (12)

For the inequality, sum the geometric series over depths d>=L and use r/(1-r)^3<=4 on [0,1/2]. Choose L large enough that (12) is below the actual positive source threshold Thr; L=100 suffices for the discussed corrected parameter settings, including Thr=1/4678. Every sibling edge joins such low-density labels. Hence H_high and H_rest are empty and H_low is the whole union of three-edge triangles. All components already satisfy the source 22-edge cap. A rule that replaces only nontrivial path components makes **zero replacements with this admissible H_low selection**. Deliberately cutting triangle edges would change the selection and require paying for the removed credit.

This proves absence of a universal positive path-component density in the source class. It is not a worst-case forcing obstruction or a hardness construction. In fact w=7 already forces each variable. For root x with children a,b, children u,v of a and s,t of b, use C_x,C_a,C_b and positive clauses (x,a,u),(x,a,v),(x,b,s),(x,b,t). If x=0 then a=0 or b=0; the corresponding child critical clause and two positive clauses contradict one another. This seven-clause witness gives the entire family a large forcing surplus. A valid surplus-aware worst-case theorem could use that escape; the family does not rule it out.

The actual source guarantee could be preserved if a lower bound on its usable forcing surplus paid every replacement loss.
