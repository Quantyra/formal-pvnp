## A concrete limit on reference constructions

Here is a mathematical observation supplied by this reviewer to the author. Suppose rank over F2 of A exceeds n and D>=3. There cannot be a genuine probability law on all Boolean X/Y/U assignments whose degree-D Gram kernel contains every bamboo gate and output residual. For an interior gate the residual is

    h = d-c-ab+2cab,

of ordinary degree three. A residual in the Gram kernel has E[h^2]=0; a genuine probability law therefore satisfies h=0 almost surely. The base gates and outputs likewise hold almost surely. There are finitely many constraints, so they all hold simultaneously almost surely. The gates then reconstruct every prefix dot product and the outputs give XY=A over F2, contradicting rank(A)>n. Fixed boundary substitutions do not change this implication.

This excludes global probability references with the whole required kernel, including attempts to repair the iid law while retaining genuine probability. It does **not** exclude an abstract truncated PSD bilinear form, compatible local distributions, or a pseudoexpectation. The distinction is essential: a truncated Gram matrix need not have a global representing measure.

In the Boolean function algebra, the full ideal of these inconsistent constraints is the unit ideal: at each Boolean assignment at least one residual is nonzero, and its point indicator is a multiple of that residual; summing the indicators yields 1. Therefore full-ideal intersection with low-degree polynomials is inappropriate. A legitimate truncated relation space must restrict the permitted multipliers/derivation degrees before taking their span, with source-support bounds checked. This observation does not assert that 1 has a low-degree derivation.

## What a quotient comparison must still show

A quotient construction must establish that every declared relation annihilates all degree-D partners under the original R, using local source support and linearity, before descending its bilinear form. A zero quadratic value alone is insufficient because positivity is the unproved target. The projected reference must retain normalization and product consistency: entries attached to two monomial factorizations of the same Boolean product must agree if the reference is asserted to be a moment functional. An arbitrary PSD matrix on the quotient need not satisfy either property. If used only as a comparison form, this must be stated and the comparison with the actual R proved.

The elementary sufficient comparison condition is exact agreement on the discarded kernel together with a relative bound |E(v,v)|<=eta H(v,v), eta<1, on a positive reference form H on the retained space. Then H+E is PSD there. Choosing an arbitrary Euclidean quotient metric makes H positive by definition but does not supply this inequality. Nor does dividing a projected Gram by its constant entry preserve a previously asserted small perturbation estimate automatically.

These are conditions on the proposed mechanism, not a construction of the required asymptotic PSD extension. No X-only theorem or exponential-row negative square is being repackaged as resolution of that target.
