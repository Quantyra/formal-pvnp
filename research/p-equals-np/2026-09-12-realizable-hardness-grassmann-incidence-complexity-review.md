coordinate space `(Fin J x Fin 3) -> ZMod 2`. Its finite instance follows
from finite submodules via their underlying sets, not an externally supplied
list of purported advice objects. Each fibre filters this full finite type
by actual containment in `retained d`.

The selected coordinate is retained for every block choice. The explicit
linear embed places the first a scalar coordinates on selected coordinates
of distinct blocks. Under a <= J it is injective; its range has dimension a
and lies in the retained subspace. Thus fibre nonemptiness and positive
cardinality are derived geometrically, including a=0 and J=0. The same
construction proves retained dimension at least J. The uniform kernel is
exactly reciprocal actual fibre cardinality on containment and zero off it;
normalization follows by summing that constant over the actual fibre.

The prior is the product law of the existing triple draw: none has mass
1-beta and each of the three singleton choices has mass beta/3. Joint mass
is this prior times the constructed uniform kernel. The marginal and
conditional instantiate the accepted finite Bayes definitions. Consequently
the module supplies the concrete draw/Q experiment and exact atomwise Bayes
identity required before estimating its posterior likelihood. It does not

## Quantifier and probability boundaries

- The a <= J premise is sufficient to make every draw fibre nonempty. It
  is stronger than the draw-specific a <= dim(retained d), but appropriate
  for this intended regime. The final parameter theorem must establish it;
  the source does not establish eventual a <= J from manuscript parameters.
- Normalization identities hold algebraically for every rational beta.
  They are probability laws only with 0 <= beta <= 1, as required by the
  nonnegativity lemmas. A signed normalized sum outside that range must not
  be used as a probability. Endpoint examples are legitimate.
- `conditional_normalized` and `bayes_joint` require positive advice
  marginal. At a zero marginal, rational division makes every conditional
  atom zero; this is not a normalized conditional distribution. The ratio
  theorem requires positive prior atom, but not positive marginal because
  it is an algebraic identity using total division. Its probabilistic use
  still requires a positive marginal and the beta range.
- The random variable is the full block-choice record d. The geometric
  space is retained d. The kernel already depends on d only through that
  space and its actual fibre, but the manuscript's atomwise notation for V
  still needs a proved identification or finite pushforward argument. Do
  not silently replace record-atom probabilities by geometric-atom ones.
- Conditioning changes the draw law. No conditional independence is
  asserted. No function W(d) or adaptive rank estimate is smuggled into
  these theorems; later W(Q) must be fixed after Q before applying the
  unconditional arbitrary-subspace rank bound plus posterior density.
