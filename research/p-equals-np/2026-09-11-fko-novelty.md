# FKO claims: prior-art and novelty assessment

2026-09-11; S3068. A focused primary-source audit under the [integrity boundary](../../INTEGRITY-CLAIMS.md), covering S3064-S3067. This assesses novelty and significance separately from the earlier proof reviews. It is not a manuscript, publication approval, priority certification or patent search. No experiment or new theorem is introduced here.

**Conclusion:** the sign-filtering, signed-walk/trace, parity-extraction and elementary accuracy calculations are established ideas or routine adaptations. The S3066 capped focused-growth failure statement is the only plausible narrow theorem candidate for which this focused search did not locate an exact prior statement. That is weaker than established novelty. Its proof uses standard tools, and its cap excludes an important behavior of the published algorithm. Current evidence does not justify starting a paper around the combined work; S3066 could warrant a targeted scholarly validation of priority and significance, rather than packaging the other calculations as contributions.

## Close comparison of the global-walk claims

The closest direct estimator is not an unrelated quantum analogy. Cohen-Steiner, Kong, Sohler and Valiant give `ApproxSpectralMoment`: choose a uniform vertex, run a fixed-length walk, and average return indicators. Section 3.2 derives the normalized trace identity; Lemma 4 gives additive accuracy from Hoeffding. Their Theorem 1 concerns normalized spectral-distribution approximation, not a pointwise upper certificate for the largest eigenvalue. S3067 preserves that basic estimator and adds signed channel products, a cemetery state and diagonal similarity. These are legitimate adaptations, but no improved statistical guarantee accompanies them. [Primary algorithm and lemma](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1712.01725).

For the rooted construction, the source supplies C_ref, G_ref, the rational floor, mutual exact-two retention and H_ref=Gamma^(-1/2)C_ref Gamma^(-1/2). S3067 uses the similar matrix Gamma^(-1)C_ref so channel probabilities fit a killed walk. Similarity preserves the trace, while retaining each channel's original two clause IDs preserves extraction information that an aggregated matrix entry could hide. The input-dependent normalization is not a new operator. Neither the source's refutation certificate nor its defect accounting has been made cheaper by this adaptation. [Schmidhuber-Hastings v1, equations 9.3-9.10 and Figure 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.29672v1).

The factor 4p in the extracted tuple is exact bookkeeping: two clause labels per step, followed by reduction modulo two. No extra search theorem is hidden in that identity. A positive walk can reduce to the empty set, and a negative walk does not guarantee useful clause coverage across later outputs. The existing signed double-cover construction also represents a negative closed walk as an opposite-sheet path. This makes the mechanism familiar rather than establishing a new certificate algorithm. [Brown et al., Section 5 and equation 25](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.0505).

The accuracy warning is valid but must not be oversold. Later work by Jin, Musco, Sidford and Singh proves limitations for moment-based spectral-distribution approximation and specified random-walk access. Those theorems concern other input families and a Wasserstein target; they do not prove our particular rooted operator requires the S3067 sample count. The contribution of our calculation is identifying the wrong accuracy contract in one proposed reduction. [Jin et al., Introduction and main theorems](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v195/jin23a/jin23a.pdf).

A stronger competing trace technique is important to this judgment. For PSD A, Hutch++ obtains a relative trace estimate using O(sqrt(log(1/delta))/epsilon+log(1/delta)) matrix-vector queries and charges additional ambient-vector work. Taking A=H^(2p) does not give free queries: applying A to a vector must still be implemented. Conversely its relative-error theorem means our inverse-N additive-return calculation cannot be called an unavoidable barrier for every trace method. [Hutch++, Algorithm 1 and Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.09649).

The rooted source requires a bound sound on every input and every rooting. A random upper-confidence estimate alone has a failure event on a fixed input and does not replace that proof. This distinction is an application of the existing certificate contract, not a newly discovered objection to probabilistic numerical analysis.

## The one tentative theorem candidate: S3066

S3066 has more mathematical content than a numerical substitution. It explicitly maintains independent uniform unused endpoints conditional on the permitted coarse adaptive transcript, counts first creations of clauses with at least two exposed endpoints, and derives an exponential tail through stopped conditional moments. It then shows a dependency of e clauses needs at least e/4 such creations and combines the known minimum-size event with fresh virtual restarts. This is a specific theorem, with a proof, rather than only an unsupported statement that adaptive conditioning is difficult.

Nevertheless its scope is materially narrower than the practical FKO algorithm in Wu et al. The cap bounds the whole explored prefix, not just the output core. A method can explore a larger subformula and prune to a small dependency; S3066 does not rule that out. Its restart rule forgets failures and does not rank choices using unused endpoint labels. The theorem also does not cover arbitrary input-aware seed selection, accumulated Gaussian state or a global lookahead strategy. These limitations should be visible in any title or abstract, not buried in a footnote.

No exact prior theorem matching all these conditions was located in this focused audit. The proof components are standard, however, and an exact statement absent from search results may still be an immediate application known to specialists. Correctness review alone does not establish a new technique. The theorem's possible value is explaining why a natural but strongly capped implementation does not find the known short structures; it does not improve a refutation algorithm, constrain the published uncapped method, or resolve the FKO discovery frontier.

A possible broader information-restricted exploration formulation was noticed during review. It is not a theorem claimed here, and cannot be used to inflate the significance of the current statement. Establishing a broader result would require a separately specified policy class and review, not a retrospective novelty label.

**Paper-level judgment:** retain S3066 as a tentative, narrow statement worth expert priority/significance validation if a paper is being considered. Do not treat it as already paper-ready, and do not begin a combined manuscript on the strength of the routine S3064/S3065/S3067 components. No exact-match search hit is insufficient evidence to promise publishability. No new validation campaign or manuscript is automatically activated by this assessment.

## The user's meta-graph, holography and interference idea

A persistent [research meta-graph](2026-09-11-research-meta-graph.md) now records the representations, proved transformations, restricted failures and unproved discovery edges. This is immediately useful as an evidence ledger: it prevents repeatedly treating a failed or uncharged transition as established. It is distinct from the exponentially large lifted-state graph inside a representation node.

There are genuine mathematical mechanisms behind the suggestion. Signed double covers make path-sign parity explicit. Holographic algorithms make compatible tensor-basis changes while preserving a global contraction; tractability then comes from the transformed signatures and an efficiently evaluable structure, not from the word 'holographic'. Valiant's matchgrid/Holant theorem is a primary example. [Valiant, Theorem 4.1](https://people.seas.harvard.edu/~valiant/holographic11-07.pdf).

Neither mechanism supplies a new result for our present FKO objects. A graph of research steps contains noninvertible or conditional arrows and is not automatically a unitary transition system. A physical quantum implementation would need charged state preparation, coherent transitions, time/accuracy and a detectable, verifiable output. A holographic route would need simultaneously tractable transformed constraints and topology, with effective basis discovery and contraction. Those remain explicit unproved edges in the ledger; cancellation alone does not provide a certificate finder. Physical holography is not invoked as a complexity theorem.
