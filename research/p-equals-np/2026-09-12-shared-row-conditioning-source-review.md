## Source contract that conditional constructions must retain

Garlik--Gryaznov--Ren--Tzameret,
[TR26-133](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/133/), Definitions 6.5--6.7
and Lemma 6.4, defines uniform local pairs of augmented full-rank X and Y
matrices with the prescribed cross products, odd free rows/columns, and
actual prefix auxiliaries. Its consistency theorem compares restrictions
of these local laws. It proves the stated SA property, not the full mixed
square PSD sought here. The previous review read the cached primary text;
the same source contract applies to S3099.

At even n, m=n^2, A=I_m, a full ordinary-degree-D square is safely in the
source domain under 4D<=n-2. Typed X-row and Y-column supports count
separately. This is a monomial-by-monomial bound: a polynomial can sum
over arbitrarily many different label contexts. A positive law on one
fixed context therefore does not prove the target.

Exact source gate/output relations annihilate supported multipliers.
Removing a relation from a comparison's null space or obtaining its
zero-square value is insufficient unless the partner annihilation and
relevant multiplication identities are justified. S3094 already exposes
failure of an orthogonally projected iid form to be a moment form; S3095
already supplies signed interpolation without PSD. S3098 rejects the
particular independent-edge assembly by actual shared-row correlations.

## Exact candidate received before experiments

The author supplied the following concrete candidate, not a generic global
gluing proposal. For each typed context S of size at most 2D, define C_S p
monomial by monomial by conditional expectation under the actual source law
on S union rows(M). Set

    Delta_S p = sum_(T subset S) (-1)^(|S|-|T|) C_T p,
    Q_D(p,p) = sum_(|S|<=2D) ||Delta_S p||^2_(rho_S).

All summands have unit weight. Each conditional union has at most 4D typed
rows, so it is a valid source context under the target degree condition.
Conditional versions need only be defined on the support of the marginal;
consistency makes arbitrary values on null states irrelevant when lifted
to a larger source context. This retains the actual rank, parity, output,
and prefix constraints in every conditional law. Supported truncated
relation multiples have zero conditional evaluation, so the reference
annihilates the declared K_D. These are definitional applicability checks,
not certification of a relative comparison to R.

The proposed sum of squares of conditional differences is a positive
Hilbert form by construction. The author does not assert it is a moment
functional or a global satisfying measure. It therefore avoids the first
global-law veto above. The missing obligation is the uniform estimate
|R(p^2)-Q_D(p,p)|<=eta Q_D(p,p) with eta<1 on the full varying-context space.
Conditional operators for dependent contexts cannot simply be assigned
the commuting identities or orthogonality of product-law projections.

## Directly relevant primary precedent and failed hypothesis

[Chastaing--Gamboa--Prieur, arXiv:1112.1788v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1112.1788),
Section 2.2, presents the independent-input conditional decomposition and
its orthogonal variance identity. Their dependent-input Theorem 1 uses
hierarchical orthogonality and condition (C.2): the joint density dominates
a positive constant times the product of the two marginal densities for
every split. This is an additional hypothesis, not a consequence of local
consistency. The actual primary PDF was checked for this candidate.

That hypothesis fails even for one source X/Y pair: each odd-row marginal
has full odd-vector support, but the joint law has zero mass on the
forbidden dot-product parity, a set of positive product-marginal mass.
Consequently the cited theorem does not supply a unit-weight isometry or
the missing uniform comparison here. This is an applicability veto for
that specific import, not a veto of the author's explicit reference.

The primary paper's printed page 14 explicitly describes extension of

does not already provide the present full bamboo square-positivity
statement. No claim of exhaustive literature absence or first-ever SoS
lower bound follows from this comparison.

For clarity about a possible separately reviewed corollary, source
Definition 4.3 measures SoS certificate degree in
sum_i f_i g_i + sum_j h_j^2 = -1 by
max{max_i deg(f_i g_i), 2 max_j deg(h_j)}, before Boolean reduction.
For degree at most 2D the square roots have degree at most D. A nonzero
clause-falsification axiom f_i has positive degree and mentions at most
two typed rows. Each multiplier monomial consequently uses, together
with that axiom, at most

    2 + 2(2D-deg(f_i)) <= 4D

rows. A common source law then gives zero for that axiom multiple.
Boolean equations vanish after Boolean reduction; optional twin variables
can be replaced by complements without increasing degree. Thus a
fully reviewed theorem with these exact axiom conventions would exclude
SoS certificates of degree at most 2D. This is degree accounting, not a
certification of any size bound or other encoding reduction. The current
public candidate remains the analytic PSD statement unless an explicit
corollary is separately adopted and reviewed.
