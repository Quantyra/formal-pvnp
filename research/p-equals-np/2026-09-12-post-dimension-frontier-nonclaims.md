## Concrete operation and resources

The actual operation randomly pairs variables through a uniform permutation,
processes the blocks in induced order, removes each of four pair values
contradicted by at most a fixed D residual original clauses, and samples
uniformly from survivors. Clause-subset and assignment enumeration implement
the test; no SAT/continuation-count oracle is hidden. An odd variable is a
two-value singleton. Original satisfying assignments survive their own
prefixes, but a surviving local value need not extend to a full solution.
A successful output is checked against the original formula. FAILURE of
one run or finitely many runs is not an UNSAT certificate.

The direct cost O(Q_D(m)2^(3D+2)poly(D,L)) per table, all stages,
preprocessing, substitutions, random sampling and witness checking are
charged. Polynomial per-run work requires fixed D; choosing D as a function
of n changes that contract. A better success bound would need its own
repetition and finite-strength analysis. Retaining a Boolean PPSZ portfolio
preserves a baseline, not an improved exponent.

Grouping yields a (4,3)-CSP representation with constant-size forbidden-
tuple expansion. D original-clause witnesses can use up to 8D tuple clauses;
D tuple clauses originate in at most D original clauses. Thus equivalence
of the known operation requires constant inference-strength rescaling, not
identical tables at the same numerical D. The generic CP2016 upper guarantee
translated to Boolean variables is weaker than the reported Boolean frontier.
It is an upper guarantee, not a runtime lower bound or an upper bound on this
subclass's success. A new subclass-specific analysis remains logically possible.

disjoint events. Writing this identity names a correct target quantity;

## Complexity relevance and closeout

The operation is classical witness search. Its representation and table
compactness are not an efficient global consistency query. XOR algebra,
counting, symmetry, preprocessing and decomposition remain particular-input
escapes with their own discovery/cost obligations. No all-known-solvers
hardness, proof-system lower bound, arbitrary CNF-to-XOR recognition or
quantum advantage is asserted.

algorithmic improvement, not P=NP or P!=NP. A polynomial randomized
one-sided total-time SAT algorithm would imply NP=RP, not automatically
P=NP. The stated direct P=NP implication requires a deterministic polynomial
decision algorithm, with any derandomization/hitting-set construction
proved and charged. No such algorithm or reduction is supplied. Failure of
