## Mathematical checks

- **Soundness.** Every accepted group has all its negative binary pairs checked, so its Boolean sum is at most one. Even parity then forces sum zero; odd parity forces sum one. A matching positive clause and AMO conversely imply odd parity. Gaussian membership queries must include the right-hand side, as the implementation does. Exact-support buckets of width at most three are accepted only when their entire satisfying relation is affine. Original clauses remain; this is neither arbitrary-window hull extraction nor a complete semantic recognizer. Proportional aggregation uses positive multipliers and verifies every coefficient before comparing constants.
- **All-size interaction, k >= 4.** The residual negative graph initially gives exactly the X clique and Y pair. Distinct chain-gadget supports recover the homogeneous equations whose sum is XOR(X)=0. AMO feedback sets X to zero, the cross clauses set both Y variables to one, and their negative pair conflicts. The parity-only chain forces no individual coordinate: each proper-prefix parity and each X coordinate can vary within the even-X solution space, while Y is free. Counting-only has no units and nonproportional aggregate vectors. Thus the two specified components stop OPEN on precisely the same CNF that the hybrid refutes.
- **Fractional comparison.** Setting x1,x2,y1,y2 to one half and all other variables to zero satisfies all original clause inequalities and the discovered AMOs. The first XOR gadget point is the average of 000 and 110; later gadgets are at zero. This excludes a refutation by nonnegative linear combinations of those inequalities. It does not exclude integer rounding, extensions, other recognized inequalities, or stronger parity/counting procedures.
- **Termination correction verified.** Groups are discovered from the current residual graph, not a globally frozen graph. Only additional fixed variables can change its negative edges: learned clauses are positive. There are at most n+1 graph epochs, O(n^2) proposed/accepted supports per epoch, and hence O(n^3) possible appended positive clauses. Other productive changes fix a new bit or increase consistent Gaussian rank. This bounds saturation polynomially; repeated proposals do not establish progress. The main note charges candidate checks, clause volume, row operations, coefficients, and retained logs.
- **Remaining failure.** All triples on five variables constrained NAE are inconsistent by the two-color pigeonhole argument. Each exact-support bucket has six satisfying points and full affine hull, hence is rejected as nonaffine; there are no units or binary edges. The hybrid therefore remains OPEN. Disjoint copies preserve this failure and give a growing family. This is a failure of this policy, not a lower bound for general SAT methods.
