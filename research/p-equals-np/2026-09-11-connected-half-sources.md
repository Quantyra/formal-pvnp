# Connected-half pricing: source and complexity contract

2026-09-11; S3073, under [integrity](../../INTEGRITY-CLAIMS.md). Focused primary-source comparison for connected-part syndrome/sign/cardinality pricing. The universal two-thirds connected cap replaces the initial half-size comparator restriction; the structural companion additionally gives exact half-size unions of logarithmically many connected pieces. No experiment, implementation or novelty determination is asserted; improvement claims require the separate reviewed algorithm and its explicit baseline.

## Closest verified algorithmic ancestry

[Dumer, Kovalev and Pryadko, Distance verification for classical and quantum LDPC codes, arXiv:1611.07164v1, November 22, 2016](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.07164), subsequently IEEE TIT 63(7), 2017, gives two directly relevant constructions. Section III.B, Proposition 3, uses matching syndromes of complementary-coordinate half lists; equation (8) states the generic classical exponent h_q(delta)/2. It does not restrict halves to connected clause sets. Section V, Definition 1 and equations (33)-(37), searches irreducible codewords by extending an unsatisfied check. For binary symbols, its additively irreducible extension has one new coordinate, with at most row-weight-minus-one choices. The inspected construction retains full-weight exponential branching, not a connected-half exponent. Its regular-ensemble average-distance analysis is not a theorem about the iid FKO incidence model. No dense-code numerical exponent is imported. These sections directly establish that syndrome joins and sparse irreducible-codeword exploration are existing methods, but do not supply the exact proposed weighted comparator guarantee.

The [Patel-Regts Lemma 2.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1707.05186v2) enumeration contract was checked in S3072: all connected subsets of size at most h can be listed in O(M h^3 (exp(1) d)^h) for an M-vertex graph of maximum degree d. Its proof exposes d explicitly. This is the source of the proposed reduced half-list size, not a new enumeration theorem. Set d=max(1,3(Delta-1)) for weight-three clause supports and maximum variable occurrence Delta. Polynomial incidence construction, syndrome formation and exact original-ID retention remain charged.

The output is a symmetric difference, so overlap is already handled in that construction. Section 4.3.2 retains the full-distance connected-cluster exponent O([2(row-weight-1)]^distance). Section 4.3.3 distinguishes exact graphlike degree-two search from generally heuristic truncated searches, including use on degree-three colour-code inputs. These are not a uniform exact degree-three improvement. The relevant HTML sections and version history were read; the large PDF exceeded web retrieval limits. This current comparison makes the signed join direct prior, while the connected-list cap reduction and weighted packing application still require their own analysis. Benchmark performance is not a worst-case guarantee.

## The initial graph condition and its replacement

[Even and Tarjan, Computing an st-numbering, Theoretical Computer Science 2(3), 1976](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3975(76)90086-4) gives a linear-time construction and attributes the underlying existence result to Lempel, Even and Cederbaum. For a biconnected graph and selected edge st, s and t can be first and last while every other vertex has both an earlier and a later neighbor. Thus each nonempty prefix and suffix is connected, including a split at half the vertices. The original publisher abstract states the precise condition; repeated full publisher retrieval failed, so this note does not claim full-proof inspection of that algorithm.

This is sufficient for a circuit whose induced clause-intersection graph is biconnected. Merely connected sets do not inherit balanced halves. The separate [structural analysis](2026-09-11-connected-half-structure.md) instead pairs even row occurrences to form a connected cubic multigraph, then uses a subcubic spanning-tree centroid to obtain parts of size at most floor((2e+1)/3). That elementary argument was independently checked: a disconnected pairing component would itself be a proper dependency. It applies to every minimal weight-three circuit, so the original half-size structural-mass gap is removed at the larger cap; no random biconnectivity theorem is needed. Exact prior status of this combined algorithm remains unverified.

## Independent check of the proposed price comparison

Let the stored half family contain connected sets of size at most h. Each part is keyed by its binary incidence syndrome, sign parity and exact cardinality. Compare opposite signs only when the two cardinalities sum to at most k. For one queried nonnegative rational clause-price vector, retain a minimum-price half in each bucket. If a comparator odd even-incidence tuple T has disjoint connected halves T0,T1 in this family, their syndromes agree and signs differ. Representatives must retain those same cardinalities. The chosen representatives R0,R1 satisfy

    price(R0) + price(R1) <= price(T0) + price(T1) = price(T).

Their symmetric difference U has zero incidence, odd sign, nonzero support and size at most |T0|+|T1|<=k. Nonnegative prices imply price(U)<=price(R0)+price(R1), even if the representatives overlap. Therefore overlap is not a soundness or comparator-price obstruction. The structural theorem supplies a split for every short minimal circuit. Any odd even tuple contains an odd minimal circuit of no greater nonnegative price. Thus the price domination can compare with the full short-odd-tuple family, even though the emitted tuple need not be minimal or biconnected. This conclusion uses the separate structural theorem, not st-numbering alone.

Scanning opposite-sign cardinality pairs per syndrome, at most O(k^2) per syndrome, avoids a quadratic cross product of list items for this pricing query. It does not enumerate all witnesses. At a new price vector, cheapest representatives must generally be recomputed; retaining the previous minima would not preserve the guarantee. Precompute only price-independent supports and keys. With L stored halves and b-bit input prices, a direct rescan and exact comparison costs L times polynomial(h,b,log M), plus preprocessing/sorting and original-input overhead. At the selected h=floor((2k+1)/3), omitting cardinality would allow a support larger than k. Weight-three binary dependencies themselves have even size.

## Packing is a separate algorithmic contract

[Plotkin, Shmoys and Tardos, 1995](https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.20.2.257) is established oracle-based fractional packing ancestry; only its publisher abstract/metadata was recovered in this pass, so no exact iteration bound is borrowed here. [Young, Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1407.3015), checked in S3072, gives nearly linear arithmetic work for explicit mixed packing/covering instances. An implicitly indexed comparator family is not automatically that explicit input.

An adaptive wrapper must prove its number of price calls is polynomial in original input and inverse margin, bound price bit lengths, and charge every complete half-list rescan. The resulting dependence can remain linear in L times those factors; generic polynomial time in L does not preserve the proposed exponent. The fixed-input price comparison works for adaptive prices without claiming fresh random signs. The universal circuit reduction preserves the full short-tuple packing mass. Strict FKO threshold slack, the wrapper guarantee and exact output verification are still required. None follows merely from a successful cheap query.

## Stronger list variant: separator ancestry and distinction

The structural companion's further construction uses one branch of centroid recursion at each level to realize any prescribed side size. A subcubic tree has at most three branches per centroid; halving the recursive tree size bounds the pieces on each side by q=3 ceil(log2 k)+3. This argument was independently checked. Enumerating all disjoint unions of at most q connected subsets with total size at most h=floor(k/2) includes the comparator halves. Multiplying the source connected-set counts and summing positive size compositions yields at most poly(h,q) M^q (2 C d)^h attempts. This is not enumeration of all arbitrary h-subsets and not an assumption of a low-width decomposition of the full input graph.

The suggested trellis analogy has a different access contract: [Kashyap, Matroid Pathwidth and Code Trellis Complexity, arXiv:0705.1384](https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.1384) relates global code trellis complexity to matroid pathwidth. Only its primary abstract was inspected in this pass; no detailed algorithm bound is imported. A low-width layout for the entire code is not furnished by a small tree on the unknown witness. Neither this source nor the directly inspected cluster/bipartition sections was verified to contain the logarithmic-component half-union algorithm. Searches `LDPC distance algorithm separator`, `code minimum distance pathwidth algorithm trellis`, and `cluster matching bipartition combined` found no exact applicable theorem. This limited negative search is not evidence of priority.

The potential logarithmic cost at the FKO scale is now (1/5)k log n+O(k)+O((log n)^2), using the separately justified general-list oracle and polynomial-call wrapper. Its M^q overhead must remain explicit outside that regime. The mathematically stronger variant is now adopted in the main analysis and supersedes the two-thirds comparison; neither variant justifies a fastest-known claim from the present source survey.

## Original FKO margin recovered

The original [FKO paper, Section 4, Theorem 4.1 and Corollary 4.2, printed p. 9](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/unsat.pdf) defines robust witnesses by t>d(I+n lambda), twice the ordinary certificate threshold. Its corollary explicitly invokes robust witnesses with k=O(n^(1/5)) at sufficiently large fixed density constant. Section 3.1 explains the constant amplification: tuple supply increases linearly with the density multiplier whereas the imbalance/eigenvalue bounds increase only with its square root. Thus constant-factor approximation loss need not be assumed as a new random structural theorem. Certified upper-eigenvalue precision and the exact sampling contract still require application-level accounting.

The paper's concrete model samples signed clauses without replacement. For M=Theta(n^(7/5)) iid uniform signed clauses, collision probability is O(M^2/n^3)=O(n^(-1/5)); conditional on no duplicate signed clause the unordered distribution agrees. This elementary coupling transfers high-probability existence statements without claiming that duplicate unsigned supports never occur. It does not transfer a literature-wide fastest runtime or numerical leading support constant.
