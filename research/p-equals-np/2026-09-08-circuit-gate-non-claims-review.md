## Available-artifact findings

- The source note supplies coefficient majorants conditional on parameter and
  pressure data. It explicitly leaves outer admissibility, pressure holomorphy,
  contraction tests, and completed-field tails unresolved. A source
  neighborhood or an actual completed velocity has not been certified.
- The packet theorem assumes a controlled scalar PDE, transported masks,
  integral sensors, distributed actuators, isolated scalar species, and an
  external clock and finite schedule. Its robust Boolean gates are mathematical
  consequences within that model; they are not Navier-Stokes-only logic.
- The exporter is a separately assumed boundary-driven transmission line.
  Its latency and noise bounds do not establish a physical fluid exporter.
  Fixed positive distance and bounded signal speed conflict with the stated
  fixed-start shrinking cutoff for sufficiently large gate budgets.
- The SAT microprogram is exhaustive search with exponential gate count.
  Polynomial description length is distinguished from execution work;
  truth-independent scheduling is not presented as a free scheduler.
- The strain estimates are sufficient upper bounds, not necessary cost or
  impossibility results. Exact arithmetic checks are not represented as PDE
  certification or asymptotic tests.
- The cancelled explicit-support polynomial-calculus proposal is distinguished
  from the revised shared-circuit representation. A representation distinction
  does not grant efficient search or a uniform circuit-compression algorithm.
- The shared-circuit note gives an exact existential-elimination rule and
  limits its exponential-work counterexample to the specified normalizer and
  variable order. It explicitly notes that paired elimination handles that
  same family cheaply. It therefore does not claim an all-order lower bound,
  a general circuit-compression obstruction, or P!=NP.
- Boolean quotient identity is correctly distinguished from formal polynomial
  identity. No free semantic-zero, factorization, or quantifier oracle is
  included in the rule. Small exact examples are supporting executable checks,
  not substituted for the all-k argument or a universal complexity theorem.
- The stronger factor-aware rule and syntactic XOR pivot are expressly outside
  the raw-normalizer counterexample's scope. The connected nonlinear example
  has a proved cheap local order. Hub-first measurements illustrate lost
  decomposition, without claiming exponential growth for all orders or a
  general nonlinear obstruction. The failed "each original factor once"
  amortization argument is distinguished from a proof that every adaptive
  factor-aware algorithm fails. The needed cumulative-work invariant remains
  unproved, rather than disproved by the examples.
