## Implicit work versus global query

A state has O(ell log n) bits and its retained channels can be generated by polynomially many explicit-input scans. This avoids ambient-vector storage for one walk; it does not apply the operator to arbitrary N-coordinate vectors. With polynomial p, one walk uses polynomial expected bit work, including exact rational transition sampling. Rejection-sampling tails must either be charged or reported as capped failures. R repetitions multiply the work; dead and unsuccessful walks count. Arithmetic for binomial dimensions uses O(log N) bits, which is polynomial at the declared level, but this does not make N coordinates polynomially many.

Channel-wise killing and sign multiplication implement the signed matrix Gamma^(-1) C, similar to symmetric H. Uniform starting states and closed-walk similarity cancellation give exactly E Y=tr(H^(2p))/N. Parallel channel labels remain available for witness extraction rather than being lost through signed aggregation.

The even-moment norm bound is valid. Its worst-case dimension factor requires p of order at least log N for constant multiplicative slack; this is not an assertion about the actual random-instance spectrum. For Y in [-1,1], the stated one-sided Hoeffding radius is correct. On the specified zero-mean observed transcript, the proposed upper enclosure is below u only when R exceeds 2 N^2 u^(-4p) log(1/delta). This algebraic condition belongs to that additive-error enclosure and transcript. It is not a universal sample lower bound, a variance theorem for the rooted operator, or proof that every alternative estimator fails. Larger p and finer normalized-moment precision must be accounted for together.

A high-confidence enclosure may still underestimate on a particular input and random stream. It therefore does not meet the selected pointwise-sound refutation contract. A small delta changes probability, not deterministic implication. Independently verifiable odd tuples or an actual spectral majorant would have a different and adequate soundness route, but neither is supplied by a confidence interval alone.

## Witness and complete operator accounting

Each channel contributes two same-root original clauses. Along a closed walk their residual symmetric differences cancel, and the repeated roots cancel within channels. Reducing clause-ID multiplicities modulo two therefore gives an even-incidence set. Negative product forces that set to be nonempty and inconsistent. At 2p steps, support is at most 4p, not 2p. This is an upper bound only: it does not prove typical support length or preclude shorter outputs.

The signed moment gives a difference between positive and negative closed-return probabilities, not a useful lower bound for distinct negative outputs. Backtracking, zero returns, duplicate tuples, overlap and normalized packing mass remain unbounded. The log-larger guaranteed support at the usual moment order is an insufficient guarantee, not an inherent tuple-size obstruction.

I directly checked [the source's Figure 3 and Lemma 9.2, equations 9.9-9.10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.29672v1). The complete certificate includes the capped-diagonal trace, omitted-edge deficit and intersecting-pair accounting in addition to the upper eigenvalue enclosure. The main text correctly leaves these global costs unresolved; locally computable rows do not eliminate them. Inference via a large Rayleigh quotient has a different output contract and does not replace this upper certificate. This review checked these source contracts, not the entire source proof.
