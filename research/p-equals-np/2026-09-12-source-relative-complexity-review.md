No novelty is established by this application.

## Verified source-classified zero-path witness

The final appendix remedies the earlier purely hypothetical all-triangle concern with a complete finite family. For L cyclic groups of three variables, the indexed critical clauses point to two variables in the next group; all positive triples are included. The formula has 3L+binomial(3L,3) clauses. Positive triples allow at most two zeros, whereas any zero requires a zero in every group. Therefore the all-ones assignment is unique for L>=3.

Each variable has one original critical clause. Useful critical/critical resolvents have four distinct literals. A critical/positive resolvent can have width three, but retains at least two positive literals; two positive clauses cannot resolve. Inferences from two original clauses therefore introduce no additional critical clause of width at most three. This claim would not be valid merely by the same argument for an iterated closure; the manuscript explicitly uses the source's one-shot definition. The proof reviewer checked this point independently and corrected the initial overly strong informal statement about mixed resolvent widths before the final text was written.

The exact source label-density weight, the count bound 2^d and a geometric sum give 2/[(L+1)(L+2)] as an upper bound at every finite height. Then the chosen graph is TwoCC-free and low-density, with no high/rest edges and every triangle already below the component-size cap. Taking H_low to retain it all gives zero path replacements.
