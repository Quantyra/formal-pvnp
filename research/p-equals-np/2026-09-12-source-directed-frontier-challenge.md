## Proposed exception-leaf specialization: preliminary challenge

The proposer supplied a concrete carrier: after obtaining an explicit CMMSA list F_1,...,F_M, replace each F_j by F_j OR e_j for one fresh variable per occurrence. Keep original raw weights of total one, give each escape variable raw weight lambda/M, and normalize by 1+lambda. The proposed YES threshold is (s+lambda epsilon)/(1+lambda). This is an actual input-computable construction. It increases leaf size by one and adds M variables; it does not require discovering the honest assignment or which formulas it fails.

The mathematical obligation is a quantitative parameter match. For desired output ratio sigma' and NO satisfaction threshold gamma', require

    sigma' (s + lambda epsilon) <= sigma s,
    gamma + sigma' (s + lambda epsilon)/lambda < gamma'.

These expressions track the original-variable budget and the fraction of constraints purchasable through escapes. They are not asserted to have admissible parameters here. Rational representation, positive inverse-polynomial weights, size threshold, and normalization must be retained. The operation applies to the finite sampled list; adding one variable per possible output of a succinct sampling circuit would require a separate size argument.

Existing published parameters do not directly certify the required small completeness-error product. The displayed black-box source theorem chooses an alphabet threshold after fixing its positive completeness error; reversing that quantifier order needs justification. However, the underlying PCP proof may offer a narrower dependency audit: [Minzer--Zheng](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.23991), Claim 3.2, Section 3.3.2 and Section 5.3, displays alphabet size 2^(2h), completeness loss J epsilon_1, and an outer soundness bound using the NO gap. Whether epsilon_1 can be chosen after h,J with all other dependencies charged is an open obligation of this proposed specialization, not settled here.

The root subsequently authorized the carrier lemma and one parameter/dependency audit. This lens contributed to that analysis and is therefore not a fresh independent proof lens for any resulting new theorem.

The elementary carrier implication checks as follows. In a YES instance choose an assignment of original weight at most s and enable only the escape bits of its failed tests. There are at most epsilon M such tests, so all augmented formulas are satisfied at raw weight at most s+lambda epsilon. In a NO instance, any assignment in the proposed raw budget has original weight at most sigma s by the first displayed inequality. Its original acceptance fraction is below gamma. At most sigma'(s+lambda epsilon)M/lambda escape bits can be enabled, giving the second inequality's total acceptance bound. Normalizing every weight and the threshold by the same factor changes neither statement. This uses existence of a witness, not a procedure that finds it.

Sampling must precede this transformation: first account for its additional error eta and use epsilon+eta in the budget. For fixed source constants, inverse-polynomial error concentration and an explicit polynomial list are permissible. Thereafter perfect completeness is exact on every member of that list, not a claim that a sampled nearly perfect witness already satisfies every original test. Positive rational lambda and original weights retain polynomial encodings and inverse-polynomial lower bounds for fixed parameters; these obligations must be checked for the actual chosen parameter sequence.

Additional implementation-level mathematical conditions sent to the author: choose the sample count as a power of two, so the resulting uniform-list sampler is an exact fixed-random-bit circuit. For the learning transfer's integer block lengths, retain a polynomial common denominator in the actual constructed weights, not merely a polynomial bit length for unrelated rational denominators. These are disclosed contributions to the proposed derivation, not assumptions that the final artifact has already met them.

## Persisted outline checks before actual-file review

The following records material feedback on the author's proposed derivation. It is not a final proof verdict and does not substitute for reading the complete saved proof.

1. **Conditional outer-question distribution.** The author proposed retaining the correct posterior law when conditioning a sampled d-dimensional subspace to contain a fixed r-dimensional Q. For an outer coordinate space V, the likelihood factor is the Gaussian-binomial ratio [d choose r]_2/[dim(V) choose r]_2, with the indicator Q contained in V. This agrees with the posterior induced by first sampling a uniform r-dimensional subspace of V. Treating V as unconditioned at this step would be incorrect.
2. **Controlling a Q-dependent zoom-out.** In the proposed repair, the number D of dropped blocks has mean beta J. Restrict D to at most h^4, discard Q for which the posterior tail is large using Markov's inequality, and separately discard Q where the marginal probability is below half its uniform value. Since dim(V)=3J-2D, the good-Q posterior/unconditional density ratio is bounded by a constant times 2^(2r h^4). For a fixed good Q, its chosen W(Q) is fixed before drawing V. Thus an unconditional rank-failure bound for this W can be transferred by the density bound. A union bound over nonzero combinations of the c defining rows gives at most (2^c-1) beta before conditioning: each nonzero combination has a fixed nonzero coordinate that must be lost. This is the legitimate order of quantifiers. The author's complete argument must specify all discarded fractions and constants.
3. **Further conditioning on W.** On the full-rank event codim_V(W intersect V)=c, the probability of a d-dimensional subspace containing Q also lying in W is [dim(V)-r-c choose d-r]_2/[dim(V)-r choose d-r]_2. The proposed argument treats this as 2^(-c(d-r)) with a small finite-dimension correction. This can control the extra posterior change, provided the correction and exceptional event are bounded before normalization. A mere unconditional covering estimate would not suffice.
4. **Repetition and cost.** The proposed double-exponential-in-h repetition keeps h fixed relative to the original SAT input size. It can make beta extremely small while beta J is of order h^2. This plausibly strengthens the displayed covering errors and outer soundness simultaneously, but every later proof use of J must be mapped. An enormous fixed polynomial exponent is allowed for each fixed leaf bound; no claim of useful runtime or uniform growing-parameter efficiency follows.
5. **Learning and arithmetic.** The learning-transfer contract preserves completeness error, including zero, with constant-factor ratio and logarithmic advice overhead. Its block-length implementation needs a polynomial common denominator for the particular constructed weights. Use a rational or integer lower bound for any displayed algebraic hardness factor before choosing lambda; an irrational display such as K^(1-1/m) is not itself a rational encoded weight. The source occurrence weights, explicit sample count and fixed rational parameter constants appear compatible with this requirement, but the final construction must state it. Choose the explicit sample count as a power of two to obtain an exact sampling circuit without introducing rounding into completeness.
