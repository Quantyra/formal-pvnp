## What may and may not constitute a contribution

The N^(6/7) bound itself is not new. Prefix partitioning, amplification, quantum-walk search, fixed-point preparation and reversible sorting are established ingredients. The new-to-this-route work makes the arbitrary-key obligations explicit: a deterministic sampling-density floor, a complementary bound for useful heavy fibers, and a tuple transition with paid scans. Those arguments are elementary building blocks assembled to remove the original distributional assumption. Correctness review establishes that the displayed construction works within its model; it does not establish that this extension was previously unknown or difficult.

The source's own broad claw-reduction language creates a particularly important priority risk: the authors may have intended the generic result, even though the inspected random-composition justification does not explicitly handle our repeated fibers. It would be inaccurate either to call our result a new exponent or to claim that a missing explicit paragraph proves a new theorem. Classify it as a rigorous candidate generalization of an existing low-gate method, pending a stronger prior-art determination.

The weighted application preserves an optimum through exact threshold tests and a polynomial number of adaptive calls. This is a useful correctness obligation, but standard oracle amplification and optimization wrappers do not by themselves supply paper-level novelty. Nor does multiplication by the half-list exponent establish a fastest FKO refuter: same k, witness constants, lower-order factors, access model and competing certificate methods must all match. The active-gate metric leaves idle-storage cost out and still uses exponentially many records in the FKO parameter.

## Research choice

Retain the result as reviewed route evidence; do not launch a manuscript, benchmark campaign or repeat a proof demonstration on that basis. The concrete unresolved novelty question is whether an existing same-model arbitrary-key theorem or standard promise-removal reduction already implies this complete guarantee. If deeper novelty work is authorized, resolve that exact scope through the final published body and its direct algorithmic descendants, rather than another general quantum-complexity survey. No external contact or purchase is authorized by this note. A separate substantive mechanism improving a source-supported quantitative bound would provide stronger research value than repackaging the known exponent.
