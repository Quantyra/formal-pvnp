These are elementary consequences of Boolean integer sums, not a novel generic inference rule. The source comparison supplies established pseudo-Boolean/parity proof machinery and explicitly distinguishes extensions from ordinary cutting planes. No short-proof discovery guarantee is imported from those sources.

Their OPEN outcomes and the hybrid refutation show that this feedback adds useful information to those policies. They do not separate all counting from all parity reasoning, show superiority to stronger solvers, or lower-bound arbitrary mixed inference.

It therefore certifies feasibility of that named relaxation. It is not a Boolean satisfying assignment, nor a barrier against integer rounding, cutting planes, extensions, or richer pseudo-Boolean reasoning.
