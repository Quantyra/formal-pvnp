3. **ActualFoldedRowShapes.** Import ActualFoldedParityVerifier, ActualBinaryLabelCompare, Complexitylib.Classes.PCP.Internal.UnaryList and FPBridge. Neither u nor b becomes a varying input of the claimed FP function.

5. **ActualFoldedRowEntry.** Import ActualFoldedSourceGuard and ActualFoldedRowShapes. A NO mode supplies its two entries through the ruler; a single entry does not return an entire source wire.

6. **ActualDyadicFoldedSourceWire.** Import ActualFoldedRowEntry, ActualSourceNormalization and Complexitylib.Classes.PCP.Internal.Materialize. Define ET=fstEnc composed with E and ER=sndEnc composed with E; materialize both at pair(nRows(w),w), then frame their pair. Reuse materialize_mem_FP and materialize_eq separately. Directly materializing E would encode a list of pairs and is not the required pair of lists.

The semantic list L/S and its enumeration can be defined in the guard/entry stages rather than creating a separate speculative module.
