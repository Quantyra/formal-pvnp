**Finding:** the procedure is established information-set/fundamental-circuit processing. The author's coordinate-isolation argument now supplies a pointwise survival bound, but known deterministic connected-subset enumeration already matches its degree-based exponent. ## Closest algorithm and exact success conditions

For a parity-check matrix B with greedy independent column basis J, a rejected column c has a unique representation in J. The corresponding kernel word has its only nonzero nonbasis coordinate at c. These are the rows of a systematic generator for ker B, so testing them for small weight is the one-information-coordinate form of classical codeword search. It is not a new Gaussian algorithm.

[Peters, Information-set decoding for linear codes over Fq, Section 3, pp. 3-4; document dated February 28, 2010](https://eprint.iacr.org/2009/589.pdf) explicitly describes choosing columns one by one and checking independence to obtain an information set. Its systematic-row search includes this basic operation. Section 4, p. 7 states first-iteration probabilities using a uniformly selected information set and a random weight-w error word; subsequent correlated information sets require a separate Markov-chain analysis. These assumptions do not automatically hold for a fixed planted circuit and sparse greedy pivots. No dense-code exponent or numerical work factor is transferred.

[Canteaut-Chabaud, IEEE TIT 44(1), 367-378 (1998)](https://www.rocq.inria.fr/secret/Anne.Canteaut/Publications/Canteaut_Chabaud98.pdf) is direct historical algorithmic ancestry for randomized information sets and systematic generator searches. Its author-hosted PDF was found, but repeated full-text retrieval became unreliable; no exact quantitative theorem is attributed to it here. Peters supplies the accessible detailed algorithm contract. A superficially similar recent [Yang, arXiv:2303.06889v1, Algorithm 2.1, March 13, 2023](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.06889) actually enumerates deleted-coordinate subsets and explicitly charges their binomial count; it is not a polynomial probabilistic survival theorem.

## Random greedy bases are not uniform bases

[Mayhew, Equitable Matroids, EJC 13(1), R41, April 24, 2006, Definition 1.1 and Proposition 2.3](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v13i1r41/pdf) gives an exact characterization. For a proposed basis J, impose x<y whenever y is outside J and x belongs to its fundamental circuit C(y,J). Greedy outputs J exactly when the input ordering is a linear extension of this partial order. Its probability is the number of those linear extensions divided by M!, not generally the reciprocal of the number of bases. The paper gives a counterexample and studies the special equitable class where uniformity does hold.

Thus a circuit T appears as a fundamental circuit exactly when the final greedy basis contains T minus one element and excludes that remaining element. Equivalently, all but one circuit elements survive greedy acceptance. Computing its probability involves outside columns and their ranks/spans. Merely replacing the basis by a uniform n-subset loses that conditioning. A fixed independent subset extends to some basis, but that existential fact gives no favorable random-order probability.

## Closest current aggregate matroid theorem

[Khanna-Putterman-Song, An Otilde(n^(3/7)) Round Parallel Algorithm for Matroid Bases, ICALP 2026, Theorem 1, Section 3 and Lemma 22](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol374-icalp2026/html/LIPIcs.ICALP.2026.124/LIPIcs.ICALP.2026.124.html) was checked directly. The method constructs restricted sets S with a subset-hitting guarantee: their first random-permutation circuit intersects each T subset S with probability at least |T|/(2^21 |S| log n), under its globally-optimal-set conditions. This strengthens elementwise hitting and addresses correlated circuit mass.

It does not give the probability of recovering a specified circuit, guarantee short circuit support, or apply that hitting law to the untouched ground set. Its main output is a matroid basis with improved independence-oracle round complexity, not short dependent witnesses or an FKO packing. Polynomially many queries per round are charged. It is the closest inspected aggregate tool, but applying it here would require a new shortness-and-mass bridge, not a direct import. The inspected conference text refers to a full version for some algorithmic details; no full-proof endorsement is made.

## Sparse prefixes versus planted-circuit conditioning

[Coja-Oghlan, Kang, Krieg and Rolvien, The k-XORSAT threshold revisited, arXiv:2301.09287v1, Theorem 1.2, January 23, 2023](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.09287) gives the full-row-rank threshold for iid constant-weight row supports, with its fixed positive gap below threshold. An unconditioned short prefix of iid columns can be transposed into this model. A prefix conditioned to contain all but one column of a specified dependent tuple cannot simply be called the same iid matrix. Its prescribed columns and intersections with the remaining random span require explicit analysis. The source also explains why pruning-generated dependence complicates rank proofs.

Accordingly, a proposed favorable event combining an early circuit portion with an independent prefix must charge BOTH the positional probability and the conditional rank/acceptance probability. A fixed support pattern planted in an otherwise iid formula, a random tuple conditioned to be minimal, and a tuple chosen from the observed formula are different probability spaces. Failure of one sufficient early-prefix event is not failure of the full greedy algorithm.

## Decisive existing enumeration baseline

[Patel-Regts, arXiv:1707.05186v2, September 20, 2017, Lemma 2.4, printed pp. 4-5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1707.05186) explicitly enumerates every connected induced vertex subset of size at most k in an N-vertex maximum-degree-D graph in O(N k^3 (e D)^k) time. Here e is Euler's constant. Its adjacency-list proof exposes D and applies when D grows; the claim is not merely fixed-degree notation with an unknown dependence hidden in a constant. Lemma 2.3 supplies the connected-set count. This is an enumeration statement, not the more elaborate induced-copy counting theorem.

Apply it to the graph on original clause IDs, adjacent when their supports intersect. Its degree is at most 3(Delta-1). Every minimal binary circuit is connected: otherwise its components have disjoint row supports and each separately sums to zero. Enumerating connected sets and checking parity/signs thus includes all needed short circuits. An odd even-incidence tuple contains an odd minimal circuit; replacing each weighted tuple by one such contained circuit preserves its mass and cannot increase any load. The same packing opportunity is therefore retained, not just one witness. These are elementary transfers of the existing enumeration tool, not claims made about FKO by Patel-Regts.

At Delta=O(n^(2/5)), this gives the leading scale (2/5) k log n plus O(k) and polynomial preprocessing/testing factors. The proposed priority-isolation repetition bound has the same scale. A smaller constant than the naive half-list scale (3/5) k log n consequently does NOT establish a frontier gain. Neither comparison certifies an optimal decoder or the exact fastest refuter. Subsequent explicit packing, accuracy and bit costs must be charged for both lists; no end-to-end near-linear packing theorem is proved here.

## Isolation ancestry and remaining scope

[Cai, Siu Man Chan and Siu On Chan, Random Separation, IWPEC 2006, Section 2, printed p. 242](https://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/~lcai/papers/rndspr.pdf) isolates a solution from its external neighbors by independent colors. Its displayed fair-color probability is 2^(-|S|-|N(S)|). The author's priority-threshold event is the same separation pattern with a biased threshold, followed by a linear-matroid consequence. This source does not itself state the exact fundamental-circuit bound or its constant; no exact-statement priority is claimed. The independently verified connected-enumeration baseline is sufficient to settle the present exponent comparison.

A greedy pass has polynomial matrix/provenance cost; at most M-rank(B) fundamental circuits are emitted, often long or overlapping. The pointwise bound and repeated-order union bound address completeness of the short-circuit list, not free optimization over that list. No independent-witness assumption is needed for that union bound. Sign checks and load verification remain exact operations on original IDs.

The suggested connected meet-in-the-middle refinement is only a question. Patel-Regts does not provide a partition of every circuit into two connected halves. General connected sets have a star obstruction to balanced connected bipartitions. Whether the additional minimal weight-three binary-circuit structure supports a useful decomposition and economical syndrome join has not been verified here; no D^(k/2) algorithm or source-established open problem is asserted, and no successor experiment is selected.

## Unselected restricted comparison question

The primary [Even-Tarjan, Computing an st-numbering, 1976 abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0304397576900864) states the classical numbering property for a biconnected graph: every internal vertex has both an earlier and a later neighbor. Hence every nonempty prefix and suffix is connected. This supplies balanced connected halves for a biconnected circuit-intersection graph, not for arbitrary circuits. Only the source abstract/statement was checked here, not its full algorithm proof.

A proposed pricing sketch stores a minimum nonnegative-price connected half for each incidence syndrome and sign, then pairs opposite signs with equal syndrome. Against a comparator circuit admitting balanced connected halves, the representatives have summed price no larger than those halves; symmetric difference preserves zero incidence and odd sign, cannot be empty, and only reduces price under nonnegative weights. This addresses one price query without enumerating every bucket cross product. It does not enumerate every circuit. Enough biconnected-comparator packing mass in the same random FKO ensemble, an exact algorithm/bit-cost analysis and closest-code-search comparison remain unverified. This is an unselected conditional idea, not an established improvement or a novelty claim.
