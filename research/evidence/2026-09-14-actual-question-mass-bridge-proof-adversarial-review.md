## Identity, multiplicity, and dependency audit

`rowId_incidence_card_eq_degree` is the only new mathematical bridge. Its steps preserve occurrence identity:

1. `I.rowIndices_toFinset` rewrites `Finset.univ : Finset I.RowId` as the finite set generated by the canonical row-index list.
2. `I.rowIndices_nodup` and `List.Nodup.card_eq_countP` convert the filtered set cardinality into a list count with exactly one entry per row identity.
3. `I.rows_eq_map`, `List.countP_map`, `I.support_eq`, and `ActualOccurrenceDegree.containsVar` identify that predicate with the canonical degree predicate on the generated row list.

This route does not quotient by a row's variable triple or right-hand side. Different `RowId`s are counted separately; `rowIndices_nodup` prevents one row identity from being counted twice. Repeated source owners remain harmless because occurrence anchors and row IDs retain source-slot and gadget-row identity.

The final inequality rewrites by this equality and invokes `ActualOccurrenceDegree.degree_le_four I x`. That imported theorem has the required assumption-free signature. Its construction proof separates sigma-tagged owner clouds, bounds a port by one original-row occurrence plus cloud degree at most three, and proves internal variables have degree two. The bridge neither restates nor modifies those facts.

No use of `rowPair_injective` is needed. That is correct: the target is incidence among row identities, and canonical degree counts generated row occurrences rather than deduplicated row values.
