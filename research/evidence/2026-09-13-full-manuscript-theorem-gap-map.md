Per-input reduction success is >=2/3; no exponent uniform in L is claimed.

## What the next parser and Fourier work actually buys

The strict CNF flag derivation targets exact whole-word DataEncode canonicality with a finite FP expression. Once kernel-checked it can discharge the malformed-input gate for the chosen raw CNF-to-dyadic-source implementation. It does not decode CNF records, enumerate clause/tape rows, compute truth tables, or prove that the total row producer belongs to FP. Generic final CMMSA syntax is a different codec, so this flag is not its runtime proof.

Folded verifier syntax/row correctness is different from Fourier soundness. The finite Fourier derivation is needed to prove the chosen source's NO value from the actual repeated base game. Kernel formalization would close that required source-soundness hypothesis. It does not prove the later matrix hypercontractivity theorem, smooth/advice repetition, modified star-PCP soundness or HN formula compilation. These have different distributions and quantified objects.

There is also a parameter-strength issue to preserve: the outer manuscript requires arbitrary fixed positive YES error with one absolute NO gap. Choosing one b satisfying eps<gamma proves a separated gap, but not alone that quantified family. The reviewed regularizer sandwich does allow b to be increased after any required outer YES tolerance, with fixed gamma unchanged, then u increased after eta,b. This implication must be instantiated in the eventual kernel outer-hardness theorem. Subsequent padding/smooth-game/PCP constants must retain their stated independence from that later YES error.

Thus parser/Fourier tasks are on a genuine necessary route for the selected self-contained outer-source implementation. They do not suffice for Theorem1, and neither should be reported as the last full-theorem blocker. Existing reviewed finite proofs should be formalized/assembled rather than rediscovered.
